How this is made. Every Monday at 08:00 Lisbon time, Claude reads crypto newsrooms (CoinDesk, The Block, Decrypt, Blockworks), major outlets (Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times), regulator releases (SEC, CFTC, OCC, ESMA), project blogs (Ethereum Foundation, Solana) and market data (CoinGecko, Farside, SoSoValue, DefiLlama, RWA.xyz), then rewrites this page. Each analysis lists its sources. Nothing here is investment advice.

Items marked (reported) come from press reports the company has not confirmed. Some figures reached us through daily news digests that summarize the primary report; those digests are listed as the source so you can trace them.
